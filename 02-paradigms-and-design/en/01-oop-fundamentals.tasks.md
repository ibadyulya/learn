## Question 1
Name the four pillars of OOP and, in one sentence each, what they solve. Which is
the main one and why?

## Question 2
How does encapsulation differ from abstraction? They're often confused — state the
difference.

## Question 3
Why do people say "prefer composition over inheritance"? Name at least two problems
inheritance creates, and show how the same thing is solved with composition.

## Question 4
Given `class Stack extends Array`. Why is this a bad idea from the LSP and
encapsulation standpoints? How do you do it right?

## Question 5
What is polymorphism and how does it relate to the OCP principle? Give a mini
example where polymorphism removes a `switch`.

## Question 6
In JavaScript, what is `class` "really"? What happens under the hood with
`extends`, and why aren't methods copied into every instance?

## Question 7
Privacy: how does `#field` differ from the `_field` convention and from `private`
in TypeScript? Which of them actually hides data at runtime?
